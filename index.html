About Us

Who We Are

MO ENTERPRISES is a registered sole proprietorship operating in the B2C e-commerce sector, based in Lahore, Pakistan.

We study the market to understand what consumers need, then we source the right products through wholesale channels and sell them directly to customers through our own online stores. Every brand and storefront we operate is fully owned and managed by us — we are not a marketplace and we do not sell on behalf of others.
